## I. The Epistemic Shift: From Building AI to Engineering Intelligence

The previous phase of my work proved that I could build and ship production AI. The current phase asks a harder question: 

*Can we build AI systems that know the difference between an answer, an inference, and evidence for an answer?*

This question has become the organizing principle behind my 2026 research architecture. The objective is not pessimism; it is **calibration**. A system must be able to reduce confidence when the evidence gets worse.

### The Integrity Landmark: Systems That Can Say "I Don't Know"
An earlier analysis of a large SUNDS dataset appeared to identify an association between geomagnetic activity and mortality. The signal was compelling. Then, the pipeline was rebuilt. The apparent effect disappeared—it was an artifact of data-processing, not the phenomenon hypothesized. 

Instead of protecting the hypothesis, I documented the failure and updated the analysis. This experience yielded a concrete design principle: **A system should be able to reduce confidence when the evidence gets worse.**

| Traditional AI | Evidence-Governed AI |
| :--- | :--- |
| Claim → Evidence → Insufficient → **"Probably True"** | Claim → Evidence → Evaluation → Contradiction → **UNKNOWN / REJECTED / QUALIFIED** |

## II. Research Intelligence & Knowledge Discovery

### 1. OmniMind: Research Intelligence & Latent Knowledge Discovery
Most retrieval systems answer: *"What documents are similar to my query?"* OmniMind asks: *"What meaningful relationships exist across this corpus that nobody explicitly asked for?"*

**Architecture:** `Documents → Semantic + Lexical Retrieval → Entities/Relations/Equations/Causality → Knowledge Graph → Cross-Domain Traversal → Latent Connections → Hypothesis Generation → Evidence Search → Falsification`

> **Core Hypothesis:** Vector retrieval is infrastructure. Connection discovery is the product.

### 2. The Cognitive Compiler: From Documents to Generative Capability
Conventional document processing asks what a source says. The Cognitive Compiler asks what machinery generated what the source says. It transforms natural-language knowledge into structured representations of entities, mechanisms, constraints, causal relationships, and generative processes. 

`Natural Language → Structural Extraction → Generative Machinery → Typed Intermediate Representation → Validation → Knowledge Graph → Reusable Capability`

### 3. Cangjie Deconstructor: Extracting Generative Architecture
Cangjie deconstructs technical and scientific material into reusable structural components (Primitives, Operators, Transformations, Constraints, State Changes, Causal Machinery). 
`Literature → Structure → Mechanism → Model`

### 4. Leonardo Lab: Evolutionary Architecture Discovery
Extends the compiler concept into experimental architecture search. Rather than reproducing an existing architecture, Leonardo Lab identifies the design space implied by the architecture (e.g., transformer variants like grouped-query attention) and explores alternative configurations.

## III. Evidence-Governed Technical Intelligence

### The Invention Evaluation Framework (IEF)
IEF is an evidence-governed multi-agent system for evaluating inventions, patents, technical claims, and intellectual property. It decomposes complex evaluations into propositions, maintaining explicit relationships between claims, evidence, sources, and epistemic status.

`CLAIM → PROPOSITION → EVIDENCE → SOURCE → INFERENCE → EPISTEMIC STATUS`

**Validation Architecture:**
`AUTOPROMPT → EXECUTION → EVIDENCE CONTROLLER → INDEPENDENT REVIEW → BLIND FRESH VERIFICATION → ARBITRATION → FINAL EPISTEMIC STATUS`

The system is not rewarded for making every claim come out positive. It is designed to preserve the possibility that the correct answer is *unsupported*. Current engineering includes 9 DAG lanes, deterministic claim mapping, blind fresh verification, mutation testing, and report integrity validation.

### The Three-Gate Research Protocol
A research operating system for keeping discovery from silently becoming belief.
`DISCOVER → GENERATE → GATE → VERIFY → SEARCH DROPPED THREADS → GRADE → WRITE`

## VI. Surveillance Humanism: The Ethical Architecture

The technical work is only part of the story. I also spend substantial time working with rescued wildlife, including bats. Systems are abstractions; consequences are not. This led to the concept of **Surveillance Humanism**.

People disclose things to machines that they would never disclose directly to another person. This creates a "Digital Confessional"—a stream of signals representing uncertainty, fear, illness, and crisis. 

> Surveillance capitalism asks: *How can this signal be monetized?*
> Surveillance Humanism asks: *How can this signal reduce harm?*

This research explores the boundary between behavioral intelligence and humanitarian intervention (public health, epidemiological signals, protection of vulnerable populations), treating privacy, ethics, and false-positive risk as first-order design constraints.
